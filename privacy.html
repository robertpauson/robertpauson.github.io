Privacy Policy

Screenshot Alarm (Android app app.snapalarm.clocker) · Effective 24 October 2026

Screenshot Alarm is an alarm clock for countdown timers you see on your screen. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, where it goes and how long it is kept.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Tomasz Kozlowski, Poznań, Poland, who is the data controller for the data described here. Contact: screenshotalarm@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your phone

Your alarms, notes, history, app openers, settings and the screenshots you take with the bubble are stored only on your phone. They are not backed up by the app and are deleted when you uninstall it. Reading timers from a screenshot happens on the phone first, with Google ML Kit text recognition, which does not send the image anywhere.

The accessibility service

The app asks you to turn on its accessibility service and shows what it does before you agree. The service is used only for features you start or schedule:

  • the floating bubble; when you tap it, the service takes a screenshot of the current screen so the app can read the timer on it;
  • reading the name of the app and window in the foreground, so an alarm is labelled and can reopen the same app;
  • app openers, which open an app you chose at a time you set and, for apps with several windows, tap the window you picked in Recents.
  • if you turn it on in Notes, saving the text you selected or copied, or a screenshot, to a note when you hold the bubble. Notes stay on your phone.

The service does not read passwords, messages or notifications, and nothing it sees leaves your phone except in the cases listed below.

Google services in the app

  • Google Play Billing handles payments. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Google AdMob shows ads in the free version. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, device information and how you interact with ads, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the app. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Google Gemini API processes Hyper scan images only to return the timer reading, under Google's paid API terms.

Legal bases

We process data to provide the features you use and the purchases you make (performance of a contract), to prevent abuse and keep accounts for tax law (legitimate interests and legal obligations), and for personalised ads only with your consent.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the service providers named above (Cloudflare for hosting, Google for payments, ads and AI reading), who process it for these purposes. Some of them may process data outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards they provide, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, or object to its processing, by writing to screenshotalarm@gmail.com. Because the server does not know who you are, please include something that identifies your data, such as a Google Play order number or your community name. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your phone. You also have the right to complain to the Polish data protection authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or the authority in your country.

Children

The app is not intended for children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
