Privacy Policy

Screenshot Alarm (Android app app.snapalarm.clocker) · Effective 26 October 2026

Who is responsible

Tomasz Kozlowski, Poznań, Poland, is the data controller. Contact: tomekozlowski@gmail.com.

On your phone

Your alerts, notes, screenshots and settings stay on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app. Timers are read on the phone.

The accessibility service, which you turn on yourself after an explanation in the app, is used only for features you start or schedule: the floating bubble and its screenshots, naming an alert after the app it came from and reopening that app, app openers you set, and saving text you select to Notes. It does not read passwords, messages or notifications. What it sees stays on your phone unless you send it as described below.

What we process

  • A random app ID created on your phone. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account.
  • Screenshots you send for an AI timer reading, only when you use one. They are processed to return the result and are not stored. We keep a record that a reading was used, for your balance.
  • Purchases: the Google Play purchase details, to deliver what you bought. Payment details are handled by Google Play and never reach us.
  • Communities: the community name, member IDs, and the alerts and screenshots a leader sends to members.
  • Usage statistics: which features are used, for example alerts set, screens opened or purchases started, linked to the random app ID, to find problems and improve the app. Never screenshots, texts or app names. You can turn this off in the app under MORE; turning it off deletes what your phone sent.
  • Technical data such as your IP address, used briefly to prevent abuse.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID, device information and approximate location from your IP address. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change it in the app. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Why we may process it

To provide the features and purchases you ask for (contract), to keep the service secure and improve it with statistics you can switch off (legitimate interests), to keep tax records (legal obligation), and for personalised ads only with your consent.

Who receives it

Only service providers that host our server and handle AI readings, payments and ads for us, including Google. We do not sell data. Some providers may process data outside the EEA under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • AI reading screenshots: not stored. Records of readings used: up to 4 months.
  • Community alerts and their screenshots: 3 days. Inactive members: removed after 30 days.
  • Usage statistics: up to 7 months.
  • Abuse-prevention data: up to 7 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax law requires.
  • Your balance and app ID: while you use the app.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, or object to its use, by writing to robertpattuson@gmail.com. Please include something that identifies your data, such as a Google Play order number or your community name. You may also complain to the Polish data protection authority (UODO) or the authority in your country.

Children

The app is not intended for children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes

Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
