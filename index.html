Screenshot Alarm

An alarm clock for the countdown timers you see on your screen. Tap the bubble, and the app reads the timer and rings when it runs out.

  • Set an alarm from any screen with one tap on the floating bubble.
  • Reads timers on your phone; Hyper reads hard ones with AI.
  • App openers bring back the app you need at the time you choose.
  • Communities let a leader ring every member's phone with one alert.
